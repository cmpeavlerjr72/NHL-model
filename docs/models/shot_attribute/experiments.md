# Shot attributes (location and type): experiments (append-only)

Cascade stage 4 (FRAMEWORK_PLAN section 1, item 4). Given that a team takes a shot attempt (stage 3, A3g-c, D20), this model decides where it comes from and what type it is. Rebound and rush flags are generated by the rebound / faceoff stage and are only reported here as segments. The shooter is the player layer. Stage 2 (deployment) is deferred, so SL-R1 is team-level.

---

## SL-R1: shot location x type bake-off (pre-registered 2026-10-16 by the PM, before any run)

### Question

Which model gives the best held-out likelihood of an attempt's location cell and shot type, without level, seasonal, strength-state, venue or recording-era bias, and with team responsiveness in shot quality that matches reality (L24)?

### Step 0: data audit (binding structural choice, decided by the pre-registered rule below)

Report on training seasons only:
1. **Blocked-shot coordinates.** Are they the shot origin or the block point? Compare blocked vs missed vs SOG distance distributions by strength state, and check, where shifts allow, the distance between the blocker's and shooter's recorded positions. Also report whether `shotType` is recorded on blocks, and at what share.
2. **Shot-type vocabulary by season**, with any label that appears, vanishes or shifts by more than 2 pp between seasons (L13-style recording drift).
3. **Arena distance bias.** Mean shot distance by arena relative to the both-teams expectation, per season, with SEs.
4. **Coverage:** shares of (0,0) coordinates, missing type and out-of-range points; the `sx, sy` convention (L14, L15).

**Rule for blocks**, fixed now:
- **(a)** If block coordinates are shot origins and type is recorded on at least 95% of blocks, the target is every attempt (Corsi), in the order attempt -> attributes -> outcome.
- **(b)** Otherwise the target is unblocked attempts (Fenwick) only. The cascade order becomes attempt -> blocked? -> (if unblocked) location x type -> SOG / miss -> goal. The block decision moves to the front of stage 5, and its model may not use location. Blocked attempts get no location in the sim.

### Target

- **Location cell** in the shooting team's frame (`sx, sy`, L14). Distance from the goal line centre is banded {0-10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-45, 45-64, >64 ft}, crossed with |angle| {<= 30 deg, > 30 deg}. One more cell takes everything behind the goal line. That gives 13 cells.
- **Type**, collapsed to six classes: wrist + snap; slap; backhand; tip-in + deflected; wrap-around; other. If step 0 finds drift in wrist vs snap, the collapse already absorbs it. Any other drifting label is reported, and the collapse is NOT changed after the audit.
- **Joint category**: cell x type. Every arm is graded on the joint.

### Shared inputs

- The **strength group** is ES (5v5 + 4v4 + 3v3), PP-for, PK, EN-for or EN-against. It is taken from stage-1 state, pre-event.
- The **pre-event score diff bucket** is the SA-R1 bucket set.
- **As-of team shot-quality ratings.** For and against, per strength group (ES, PP, PK). Each is the shrunk mean of a fixed reference expected goals per attempt (xG_ref, below), as a log ratio to the snapshot league mean (L1). The prior strength comes from {300, 1000, 3000} attempts and the carry weight w from {0.5, 0.7, 0.85, 1.0}, tuned on fold 1. The fold-1 offline quintile-spread ratio is reported for every grid cell (L24 guard). `created_at < puck_drop` is asserted.
- **xG_ref.** This is a lookup: the empirical goal rate per (cell x type x strength group), from fold-train seasons only, frozen per fold. It is a yardstick for the bias checks and for the G3 goal stub, NOT a stage-6 model.

### Arms

| Arm | Model | Features |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | empirical joint frequency | strength group x period type (regulation / OT) |
| L1 | factorised multinomial logit: P(cell) x P(type given cell) | L0 + score bucket + home + rest / back-to-back + own shot-quality-for rating + opponent shot-quality-against rating (row's strength group) |
| L2 | L1 + venue | L1 + an as-of arena recording factor: a shrunk log ratio of mean recorded distance in that arena to the both-teams expectation, from past games only |
| L3 | LightGBM multiclass on the joint | L2 features + game clock + period; num_leaves {15, 31}, min_data_in_leaf {200, 800}, lr 0.05, early stopping on a time-ordered fold-1 inner split |

Complexity order: L0 < L1 < L2 < L3.

### Folds, metric, noise floor, decision rule

- **Folds:** as in CLAUDE.md. Fold 1 tunes; fold 2 (2024-25) selects. 2025-26 is sealed.
- **Primary metric:** the fold-2 joint multinomial log-likelihood per attempt, as a difference vs L0. Report it per game as well (the sum over the game's attempts). One grading script, `scripts/grade_shot_attribute.py`, scores blind with hashed arm labels.
- **Noise floor:** a game-block bootstrap (1,000 resamples) of paired per-game differences, plus an L3 retrain under seed 2.
- **Decision rule (as in stage 3):** an arm beats a simpler one only by more than 2 SE and more than the seed floor. Only arms that pass every bias check and the responsiveness band are eligible. The selected arm is the most complex eligible arm that beats every simpler eligible arm. Ties go to the simpler arm.

### Bias checks (fold 2 gates; fold 1 is reported as a diagnostic)

- **Mean xG_ref per attempt**, predicted / actual: within +/-2% overall, and within +/-3% per strength group with n >= 2,000 attempts. By month, no month beyond +/-4% at n >= 100 games.
- **Location tier shares** (high danger = cells inside 20 ft and <= 30 deg; medium = 20-45 ft; low = the rest): each within +/-1.5 pp (gates.yaml g3_mix_pp), overall and per strength group.
- **Type shares:** each class within +/-1.5 pp overall.
- **Per arena** (reported; label any arena with n < 1,500 attempts UNDERPOWERED): the predicted-minus-actual mean distance. It gates only for L2 and L3, where the venue term must remove the arena slope: the regression of residual on arena factor has |slope| <= 0.3 of the L0 slope.

### Responsiveness

Teams are bucketed by as-of shot-quality-for rating quintile, and separately against. The predicted Q5-Q1 spread of mean xG_ref per attempt, over the actual spread, must lie in [0.8, 1.2] for ES. PP and PK are reported, and labelled UNDERPOWERED where the CI is wider than the band.

### Segments reported (no gate)

Rebound attempts (same team, <= 3 s after a prior attempt, no stoppage), rush attempts (<= 4 s after a neutral- or defensive-zone event of the shooting team), 3v3 overtime (INV-35 context), playoffs, and per team.

### Closed loop (G3, then G2 re-check)

- The selected arm and the next simpler eligible arm sample attributes in `sim.py` behind a default-off flag.
- The goal stub moves from goals-per-attempt by state to xG_ref per sampled (cell x type x state). Stage 5 does not exist yet, so the stub plays SOG / miss / goal as one draw.
- Stage 1 = H3''-LC2 + T2, stage 3 = A3g-c. 200 seeds.
- **G3:** xG_ref per attempt within 3% (g3_xg_rel) and location tier shares within 1.5 pp, by strength state and by team (team UNDERPOWERED-labelled), with a decomposition into attribute mix vs state visits.
- **G2 re-check:** every non-3v3 G2 cell still PASS and CLEAN, because the new stub changes scores and hence score states.
- **Under rule (b)**, the sim draws block first with the empirical block rate by strength group, labelled a stub for stage 5.

## SL-R2: as-of league mix level (pre-registered 2026-10-16 by the PM, before any run; D23)

Everything not listed is as in SL-R1: Fenwick target (rule b), 13 cells x 6 types, xG_ref fixed per fold, folds, grading script, noise floor, bias checks except the arena gate, responsiveness band, segments, and closed loop (incl. the block-first stub and the G2 re-check).

### Step 0: mix-level constructions (level-only arm L0m; no other arm is fit until the selection is written into the report)

The level is a per-(strength group in {ES, PP, PK}; EN pooled with ES) vector of as-of league log shares over the 13 location cells. It enters every arm as a per-cell offset log(s_asof / s_train), with coefficient 1, where s_train is the arm's own pooled training share. Type is not levelled: type shares passed in SL-R1.

| id | construction | grid |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | s_asof = (n_std + K s_prev) / (N_std + K): season to date shrunk to last season's final shares | K in {500, 2000, 8000} unblocked attempts per group |
| M2 | M1 with counts discounted by 0.5^(age in days / half-life) (the LC-2 form) | K = 2000; half-life in {30, 60} days |
| M0 | prior only: last season's final shares, constant all season | none |
| E | era intercept: shares from the training rows of the same recording era only (era 1 = 2021-23, era 2 = 2023-24 on, a GameState flag) | none |
| O | oracle: the season's own final shares. Diagnostic; never selectable | none |

- 2021-22 has no previous season, so its prior is its own final shares (labelled, as in LC-1). Parity is judged on the genuine-prior seasons only.
- E is admissible because the 2023-24 step is visible within fold 2's training seasons. For fold 1, era 2 has no training rows, so E equals M0's pooled fallback there and is labelled.

**Parity (per genuine-prior training season, both folds), for L0m:**
- ES high-danger share within +/-0.5 pp;
- mean xG_ref per attempt within +/-0.5%.

**Selection:**
- If any construction passes, take the simplest passer (M0 < E < M1 < M2; within a family, the smallest max |parity|).
- Otherwise take the smallest max |xG_ref parity|, with ties within 0.2% going to the simpler. List the failure and proceed (the LC-2 rule and its stated reason).

|---|---|
| L0m | L0 + level |
| L1m | L1 + level |
| L3m | L3 + level (as init_score per class) |
