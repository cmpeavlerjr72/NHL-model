# Learnings

PM-owned, append-only. Each entry ends with "For the sim:". Entries L1-L12 are imported from the sister projects; NHL-native entries start at L13.

- **L1 (CBB L2) League-mean drift.** Rating scales drift season to season. For the sim: centre every rating on its own snapshot's league mean.
- **L2 (CBB L3/L10) Shared game latent.** Independent team draws make SD(margin) = SD(total). For the sim: both teams share a game-level latent (pace/event rate); check score correlation in G5.
- **L3 (CBB L23/L27) Score-state feedback.** A post-event score feature tripled margin variance in a closed loop. For the sim: pre-event state only, and a closed-loop gate for any engine-fed feature.
- **L4 (CBB L32/L44/L45) Train/serve skew.** For the sim: every retrain passes a parity stage proving the served feature equals the trained feature.
- **L5 (CBB L22) Silent zero fills.** For the sim: feature builders raise on a missing column; never fill 0.
- **L6 (CBB L41) Binary features.** For the sim: feed a 0/1 tree feature as a draw, never as a probability.
- **L7 (CBB L38) Shrinkage compresses spread.** For the sim: compare prediction SD to realised SD, not just MAE.
- **L8 (CFB INV-45) Leaks look like edges.** Beat close 64% with zero CLV was a post-game feature joined one period off. For the sim: leak test every external feed; presume leaked any edge without CLV.
- **L9 (CFB) Settle at the posted line.** De-vigged fair lines created phantom pushes on 45% of sides. For the sim: grade at the posted book line.
- **L10 (CFB/CBB) Verified finals only.** pbp-accumulated scores were corrupt; 54 unplayed games slipped into a graded set. For the sim: two-source verified finals table.
- **L11 (CFB) Sample weights can be colliders.** Drive-proportional weights inflated INT 2.1x. For the sim: no event-count-proportional sample weights on per-event models without a check.
- **L12 (CBB L43) Workers misjudge time.** For the sim (process): every brief requires reading the clock from `Get-Date` and never ending a turn with a job running.
- **L13 NHL event recording drift.** Giveaways 15.6 -> 29.9/game and takeaways 14.0 -> 9.5 between 2023-24 and 2024-25 (recording change); SOG 57.6 -> 47.9/game while goals hold 6.1-6.4 and attempts 114-127 (blocks and misses up). For the sim: shot-type and discretionary-event rates are season-specific; league-mean centring per snapshot is mandatory, and pooled-season targets are banned in gates.
- **L14 Coordinates flip by period.** Raw x follows `homeTeamDefendingSide`, which flips every period. For the sim: use `x_norm/y_norm` / `sx, sy` from the events table, never raw x/y.
- **L15 Blocked-shot zone is the blocker's.** BLOCK events are owned by the shooting team, coordinates in the shooter's frame, but `zoneCode` is relative to the blocking team. For the sim: never use `zone` for blocks.
- **L16 Goalie GA excludes empty-net goals.** For the sim: goalie save props and goalie talent use GA ex-EN; game totals include EN goals.
- **L17 NHL scores are negatively correlated and over-tied.** 2024-25: home/away score correlation -0.115 and a 20.8% regulation-tie rate. Team-rate Poisson gives -0.04 and 15.9%, and the positive-dependence families (bivariate Poisson, shared gamma latent) cannot represent negative dependence at all (their fits hit the bound). The cause is game-state behaviour: score effects, play late in tied games, and empty-net goals. For the sim: the event engine has to produce these from pre-event game state (score effects in shot generation, a goalie-pull model, and late-tie behaviour). G5 correlation and G7 regulation-tie rate are the gates that catch it.
- **L18 Odds feeds mix pregame and in-game prices.** ESPN core `/odds` returns an "ESPN Bet - Live Odds" item beside the pregame one (e.g. BUF -3000 in-game). For the sim: every lines ingester whitelists the pregame provider and fails if it can't tell which item is pregame. A "close" taken from a live item would fabricate CLV.
- **L19 Historical "as-of" pages can be rebuilt from current data.** Daily Faceoff date pages for 2022 show today's goalies and team names (Lane F3). For the sim: a third-party historical page is never treated as pregame-known until a spot-check against known facts passes.
- **L20 Delayed penalties cause goalie pulls.** About 80% of penalties recorded with a goalie pulled follow a delayed-penalty signal. The penalty causes the pull, not the reverse. For the sim: a penalty's pre-event state is the state at the signal, or a sim that never pulls loses about 6% of penalties.
- **L21 A season dummy cannot extrapolate.** 2024-25 had 8% fewer penalties than 2023-24, and a last-training-season dummy over-predicted every arm by 10% (SS-R1). For the sim: league levels drift (L1, L13), so rate models carry an as-of in-season league level, not a season indicator.
- **L22 Penalty rates are seasonal within a season.** October runs high and January low (SS-R2: January +11% in every arm), and a season-to-date level inherits October's excess. For the sim: as-of league levels need a de-seasonalised exposure or a season-phase term, and bias is checked by month, not only overall.
- **L23 A cross-season profile cannot fix a one-season dip.** The SS-R3 profile removed October/January bias on fold 1 but left fold-2 January at +8-9%. For the sim: seasonality terms fix the recurring shape only. A residual single-season deviation is listed and monitored, not chased with fold-2-informed respecs (forking paths).
- **L24 An aggregate pass can hide over-response.** SS-R3 passed G1 cleanly at the league level, yet its team-rating quintile spread was 1.5-1.8x actual. For the sim: every gate pass is followed by the responsiveness table in the closed loop, and too steep a slope counts as a defect just as a flat one does.
- **L25 Historical third-party pages can carry today's team names.** DRatings labels pre-2024 Arizona games "Utah Mammoth" (246 games unmatched until remapped). For the sim: every team-name join to history uses an era-aware map and reports an unmatched-row count.
- **L26 A free archive can stop mid-season without notice.** SBRO 2022-23 ends at 2022-11-27 (342 of 1,400 games). For the sim: check each season's max date against the schedule before trusting its coverage.
- **L27 A ratio-to-last-season level is biased under a trend.** Entered as a fixed offset over cells pooled across training seasons, the cells absorb the mean of the training seasons' rates, and the test season is off by the gap between that mean and last season's rate (SA-R1: -5% on attempts). For the sim: league levels are absolute as-of rates with coefficient 1, and the parity check is per training season, not pooled.
- **L28 Season-to-date levels inherit early-season profile error.** For a strongly seasonal process (penalties), an error in the October de-seasonalisation persists in the season-to-date level for weeks, so the per-season parity misses by about 2% (SS-R3c). Attempts, which are weakly seasonal, pass the same test. For the sim: run the per-training-season parity test on the level alone before fitting any arm.
- **L29 Run the oracle level before blaming the level.** A constant level equal to the season's own final de-seasonalised rate passes per-season parity exactly, so any residual under it is the profile's, not the level's. LC-2's oracle exposed a profile month-shape error (October -7.5%, April +7.8% on 2023-24) that the short-memory level was partly absorbing. For the sim: every level bake-off reports the oracle row, and a short-memory level that wins should be checked for soaking up another sub-model's error.
- **L30 Never average American odds.** A mean of mixed-sign American prices is not a price (ESPN BET equal-line overs: -117.7 and +104.3 averaged to "-68", against a true centre of about -112). For the sim: summarise prices as mean implied probability, or a median American price.
- **L31 The recorded shot-location mix has an era step.** The ES high-danger share of unblocked attempts fell 14.8% -> 12.75% at the 2023-24 boundary and kept falling, misses rose 27% -> 35%, and goals per unblocked attempt held at 0.060-0.063 (SL-R1). A mix pooled across training seasons over-predicts shot quality on the next season by about 4% in every arm. For the sim: a location model needs an as-of league mix level, and the goal-given-location model needs its own conversion level, each with its own parity test, or the two stages will compensate.
- **L32 Start a booster from the stratum baseline.** A multiclass LightGBM started from the pooled class frequency and stopped early under-fits small strata. The stage-4 L3 was off in-sample on PP (-2.0% xG) and EN-for (-3.4%), pulled toward the ES-dominated mix (SL-R1, SL-R2). For the sim: when trees refine a stratified baseline, pass the log baseline as init_score, and check in-sample stratum calibration before reading any holdout.
- **L33 A baseline start fixes under-fit, not cross-stratum leakage.** SL-R3's booster (init = log stratum baseline) held ES / PP in-sample within 0.1%. But score and clock effects learned on ES leaked onto EN-for rows (all late and trailing), shifting them -0.9% where their actual mix is flat. For the sim: strata whose state is defined by score and clock (empty net, OT) get their own model or the baseline only, never shared score / clock splits.
- **L34 Likelihood cannot pick a rating's prior strength; responsiveness can.** In penalties, stage-4 location and blocks, the fold-1 LL over the shrinkage grid was flat (about 1e-4 nats per row), the chosen ratings over-responded out of sample (spread ratios 1.4-1.7), and the grids stopped at a 3,000-event prior. For the sim: extend the shrinkage grid well past the likelihood optimum and choose the cell whose fold-1 spread ratio is closest to 1, among cells within 2 SE of the best LL (D27). Amended (D29): this only works where the rating enters with a fixed scale. When the model refits a coefficient on a standardised rating, the coefficient undoes the shrinkage, and the over-response must be fixed on the response side (SB-R2).
- **L35 A rating's response can drift on its own.** After the arena content was removed from the block ratings (corr with the arena factor about 0), the per-season coefficient still fell (1.09, 0.98, 0.81). So a scale tuned on fold 1 (R 1.20) over-responded on fold 2 (R 1.33) (SB-R4). For the sim: before fixing a rating's scale from past seasons, test its per-season coefficient for a trend; if there is one, the scale needs an as-of estimate, like a level.
- **L36 A level estimated from its own judging rows always passes parity.** The era level E took its era-2 rate from the one era-2 training season and was then judged on that same season, so it 'matched the oracle' and was selected, while fold-2 goals came out at -5.2% (G-R1). For the sim: a level construction is selectable only if its parity is out-of-sample on every judged cell; otherwise it is reported and labelled not selectable (D35).
- **L37 An as-of slope fixes a drifting rating response; a fixed scale cannot.** Where the per-season coefficient of a rating trends (L35), a scale tuned on fold 1 over-responds on fold 2 (goalie R 0.70 under-responds, stage-4 "for" R 1.29 over-responds), and a daily shrunk likelihood slope on the as-of rating brings the next season's decile response to 0.98 and 1.18 with no bias cost (G-R3, SL-R7). For the sim: when step 0 shows a trend, the rating enters with an as-of slope (window H, prior P attempts, parity on the judged training seasons), never a multiplier; the per-season parity cannot be read tighter than about +/-0.1 R per season, so the [0.9, 1.1] band usually falls back to the smallest max |log R| cell, and that fallback is listed.
- **L38 A prior sized as "P events" has no strength while the ratings have no spread.** The as-of slope's prior precision is P x the per-event Fisher information, which is near zero on the first dates of the burn-in season (near-zero, collinear ratings), so the slopes spiked to 500-680 for two weeks and put the burn-in 4v4 stratum +20% (SL-R7, D42). Later season starts carry the slopes forward and show no spike. For the sim: the burn-in season is never judged or served, and any reuse of the as-of scale pre-registers a floor on the prior precision (INV-62).
- **L39 Size every offline band by a parametric redraw before pre-registering it.** Two gates on 2026-10-21 were narrower than one season's sampling error: a 5 pp limit on the shootout past-round-3 share (SE 4.9 pp on 77 shootouts, D50) and a +/-3% served / actual band per score bucket (SD 0.032 per bucket-season; an exactly right model passes all four only 20% of the time, D51). A gate below its noise floor can only fail a correct model or pass a wrong one by luck. For the sim: before a band enters a spec, redraw outcomes from the arm's own probabilities, read the 95% interval of the statistic, and set the band at 2 SE of that or wider; anything narrower is reported, never gated.
