## Step 1: construction and leakage
- z = g_r / SD_REF. g_r is the G-R1 goalie cell b 3000, w 0.5. SD_REF is the SD over the 2021-22 rows (0.0271), which only sets the unit. s_t is a one-parameter logistic slope:
  - fitted on every in-scope SOG dated before the game date;
  - rows weighted 0.5^(age/H);
  - offset = C0 (M1 K500) + the fixed team offset;
  - shrunk to the previous date's s by a prior of P shots (precision = P x the per-shot Fisher information);
  - started at s = 0, so 2021-22 is labelled burn-in and never judged.
- created_at is the previous date's last drop + 6 h, asserted < puck_drop.
- On a date when every rating is 0 (season-opening priors), s carries forward. The first run divided 0/0 there. This is a code fix, made before any result was read.
- Tests (3 pass):
  - flipping every outcome and rating on or after a date leaves s on that date and earlier bit-identical, and changes s after it;
  - the first date equals the prior;
  - shuffling rows within a day changes nothing.

## Step 2: parity analogue (training seasons only)
No cell passes [0.9, 1.1] on all three genuine-prior season cells. Every cell over-responds. Max |log R|: H365 0.497, H180 0.373, H90 0.163. The P values are indistinguishable (N_eff 30k-110k against P 2k-10k). **Selected H90 P2000** by the fallback rule (smallest max |log R|; P10000 is 0.0005 behind). Per season for H90: 1.152 (fold 1, 2022-23), 1.132 / 1.178 (fold 2, 2022-23 / 2023-24). For comparison, G-R2's fixed-scale C1 gives 0.699 / 0.685 / 1.133.

s_t tracks the drift: in raw units it goes from about -2.05 in 2021-22 to -1.73 in 2022-23 and -0.83 by the end of 2023-24 under H90. The season-alone MLEs are -2.17 / -1.79 / -0.70.

Post-hoc diagnostic, labelled and not used for any choice: using the season's own MLE as a fixed scale gives in-sample R of 1.08 / 1.06 / 0.98 (CI width about 1.1). So part of the parity miss is the 2022-23 noise floor of R, and the rest (about 0.07-0.2) is the lag of the as-of filter.
