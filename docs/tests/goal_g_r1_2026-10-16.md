Reading:
- The conversion per recorded cell keeps rising, season after season, relative to every training table.
- E and M0 lag it by a season or more. The season-to-date constructions recover most of it but still lag (-1.4% to -2.6%).
- This is the D23 / L31 drift. The recorded mix moves toward lower-danger cells while goals hold, so stage 6 sees rising per-cell conversion.
- The selection rule picked E because E's fold-2 parity is judged in sample (the D24 caveat). The rule therefore could not see the trend.

## Proposed for the PM (not edited by the lane)

- **LEARNINGS (L36 draft):** "An era level judged on its own only training season passes parity in sample, so parity cannot see a continuing trend. In stage 6, E (0.054 pp parity, oracle-equal) left fold-2 goals -5.2%, while M1 K500 (0.24 pp parity) left -1.4%. For the sim: a level construction's parity is judged only on seasons where it is out of sample, or E is excluded where its era has one training season."
- **G-R2 candidate (PM to pre-register, if wanted):**
  - Re-select the level with in-sample cells excluded. On step 1's table that is M2 H30 (0.219) or M1 K500.
  - Keep the cells, ratings and scales frozen.
  - The goalie under-response on fold 2 (0.67, UNDERPOWERED) and the step-0 goalie trend are listed. An as-of goalie scale is INV-53.
- **Watch item:** stage 6's level and stage 4's mix level are a compensating pair (D23). Any stage-6 level choice needs the closed-loop G4 / G1-goals check once stage 4 has an incumbent.
