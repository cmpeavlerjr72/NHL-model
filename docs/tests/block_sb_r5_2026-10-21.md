## Construction (as implemented)

- **Model.** B1a-as: logit p = logit B0' + (x - x_bar) . W + s_for,t zf + s_against,t za, on every SB-R1-scope row. EN rows are included, as in the SB-R4 B1a fit, and are reported only.
- **B0'** is the engine stand-in:
  - SB-R1 B0 on 5v5 / PP / PK / EN;
  - for 4v4 and for 3v3, its own M1 K500 level group plus one intercept, fitted on the fold's training rows by the engine's INV-54 code.
  - The fold-2 intercepts reproduce the engine exactly: 4v4 -1.2840, 3v3 -1.7030. Fold 1: -1.3474 and -1.7385.
- **W** is the fold's frozen SB-R4 B1a non-rating terms: 6 score dummies, home, b2b own / opp, and the arena factor.
  - The terms are centred at their fold training mean. This follows the SL-R7 template ((x - x_bar) . W), since B1a's own intercepts are replaced by B0'.
  - Labelled reading: the spec says "logit B0' + the frozen terms"; the template centres them.
- **Ratings.** zf and za are the Ra ratings of the row's rating group, each divided by its SD over the fold's 2021-22 rows (0.04687 / 0.05781):
  - own (zf) = the shooting team's blocked-against rating;
  - opp (za) = the defending team's blocks-for rating.
- **The as-of pair.**
  - Fit: a two-slope binary logistic on every attempt dated before the date, with weights 0.5^(age/H). It is shrunk to the previous date's pair with prior precision P x Ibar.
  - The AS-1 floor is applied at f = 0.5. I_ref = Ibar at s = 0 over the fold's 2021-22 rows (diagonal about 0.184; fold-2 lambda_min 0.1834).
  - Solver: the SL-R7 Newton solver (`_newton2`, imported). Every iterate is evaluated directly on the window's rows.
  - created_at = the previous date's last drop + 6 h, asserted < puck_drop.
- **Leakage tests** (5 pass, with and without the floor):
  - flip after a date;
  - first date = prior, and created_at < drop;
  - within-day shuffle;
  - zero-information dates carry the prior;
  - the floor binds only when the ratings have no spread, and damps the start.
- **Numerics.**
  - The fold-2 snapshot was computed on training rows only at step 1, then extended to 2024-25 at pred2.
  - On training dates, the extended run agrees with step 1 to at most 4.0e-16, not bit-for-bit. The inputs on the training rows are identical (checked: A, z, B0' and y are all equal); the longer row array changes the BLAS summation blocking.
  - My first pred2 asserted bit-identity and stopped before writing anything. The assert was then relaxed to 1e-10, and that is labelled here.
  - The spec-identical shuffled rerun (B1aas2) differs by at most 1.8e-16, so the seed floor is 0.
