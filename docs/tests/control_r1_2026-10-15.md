Structural findings the PM should see:
1. **Home/away scores are negatively correlated in the real data in every season** (-0.115 to -0.134 over 2021-22 to 2024-25; `gate_targets_*`). Real totals are therefore UNDER-dispersed relative to independent Poisson (total SD 2.32 vs 2.51), and regulation ties are more frequent than Poisson implies (20.8% vs 15.9%). The spec's A2 (shared component) and A3 (shared gamma latent) can only produce positive correlation and over-dispersion, so they collapse to A1. R1 cannot test the direction the data point to. Candidate for R2 (not run): a dependence structure that allows negative correlation / tie inflation (for example a diagonal-inflated or Skellam-type bivariate model, or a score-state / empty-net-aware mechanism).
2. Total-goals predictions carry almost no per-game information (total slope 0.04; SD of per-game predicted totals 0.47 vs 2.3 realised), margin predictions carry about 60%.
3. Four of the tuned values sit on a grid edge, so the pre-registered grids were too narrow: lambda = 30 (maximum of {1,3,10,30}), w = 0.5 (minimum of {0.5,0.7,0.9}), kappa = 250 (minimum of my {250,500,1000,2000}), A4 rest-term ridge = 100 (maximum of {0.001,1,10,100}). ## Implementation choices not fixed by the spec (all frozen before fold 2)

- Rating GLM: Poisson, log link, terms mu, home, offence and defence per team (ridge toward `w` x last season's final centred fit; unpenalised terms get a ridge of 1e-3 toward the previous values), refit for every game date on games with local date strictly earlier. 2021-22 has no prior (zeros). ARI and UTA are one franchise. Regulation + playoff games all enter the fit.
- Goalie term: opposing oracle starter (post-game flag; label "oracle goalie"), as-of cumulative saves / shots against across all earlier games including prior seasons, shrunk to the as-of league Sv% with kappa pseudo-shots; covariate = (league Sv% - shrunk Sv%) x 100, coefficient estimated in the GLM.
- A4 rest terms: own and opponent back-to-back (played the previous calendar day) and long-rest (>= 4 days) indicators, previous season included.
- End-game: P(home wins | regulation tied) and the OT share of OT/SO are as-of historical rates (smoothed with 10 and 6 pseudo-games); in playoffs the OT share is 1 (no shootout, a rule). An OT win adds one goal to the winner; a shootout leaves goals tied (the target excludes the SO bonus goal).
- Score pmf is exact on an 18 x 18 grid (renormalised on the regulation grid 0..16); the primary metric is analytic, so its seed SD is exactly 0. The seed floor in the table is the SD of a smoothed simulated estimate (4 offsets x 10,000 seeds) and is deliberately conservative (about 0.002 > 0.001).

## Incident: A4 numerical failure at the first fold-2 run, and the repair

The first fold-2 run (15:37:05) produced one game (2024020004, game 4 of the season) with A4 rates lambda_home 1e-8 and lambda_away 20,933: with the rest-term ridge as I had first implemented it (1e-3, effectively unpenalised) the near-empty early-season fit separated on a rest indicator. A4's pmf for that game was NaN. No score or table had been computed yet. Repair: a ridge on the four rest terms only, chosen on FOLD 1 from {0.001, 1, 10, 100} by fold-1 log score (A4 at phi = 0) subject to max lambda < 12; fold 1 itself never exploded even with 0.001 (max lambda 6.4), so the choice was driven by fold-1 score (monotone up to 100, grid edge), not by fold 2. Re-frozen and committed (71bbba2) before the second and final fold-2 run. A0-A3 are unaffected and byte-identical across the two runs (same hashes, same seeds). The spec-literal A4 therefore has no valid fold-2 result; the reported A4 is the ridge-repaired variant. A4 does not win either way (its fold-2 score is below A0's).

One presentation fix after the blind table was first printed: the table's mean column showed the bootstrap mean instead of the exact mean; I changed it to the exact mean (no number used by the decision changed).
