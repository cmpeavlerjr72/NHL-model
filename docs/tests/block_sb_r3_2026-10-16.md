## Step 0: diagnosis (2021-22..2023-24 only; 2024-25 not read)

**1. Per-season actual ES spread.** The raw Q5-Q1 spreads do not shrink clearly through 2023-24: "against" is 3.38 / 3.65 / 3.23 pp and "for" is 2.06 / 1.29 / 1.90 pp, with CIs about +/-0.8 pp. The fold-1 B1 fit is in band on its test season, 2023-24 (0.83 / 1.18). The in-sample ratios of the fold-2 B1 fit are near 1 in every season, except 2022-23 "for" (1.60, CI 0.98-5.2: the weak "for" side).

**2. Coefficient by fold.** The B1 rating coefficients barely change between folds. "for" (own_r) is 0.53 (SE 0.09) on fold 1 and 0.63 (0.07) on fold 2. "against" (opp_r) is 0.99 (0.06) and 0.94 (0.05). The refit does not create the fold-2 over-response. Fitted on each season alone, the "against" coefficient **falls every season: 1.06, 0.96, 0.85**. "for" is noisy (0.68, 0.36, 0.78). The arena coefficient also falls: 0.75, 0.57, 0.20.

**3. Within-season persistence.** The slope rises with rating maturity. The "against" slope by month is Oct 0.61, Nov 0.84, Dec 0.89, Jan 1.02, Feb 1.35, Mar 0.99, Apr 0.88 and playoffs 0.52. By exposure it is 0.72 at 0-9 games, 0.82 at 10-19, 0.96 at 20-39, 1.13 at 40-59 and 0.88 at 60+. "for" runs from 0.35 at 0-9 games to 0.77 at 60+. Rows from January on carry about 69% of the Fisher information on the ratings, so the pooled coefficient over-responds in October to December by roughly 1.2-1.5x. That share is the same in every season, so it adds a constant over-response everywhere. It cannot explain why fold 1 passes and fold 2 fails.

**4. Arena interplay.** Both ratings correlate with the arena factor: +0.19 for own_r and +0.14 for opp_r. Without the arena factor the rating coefficients rise from 0.53 to 0.68 and from 0.99 to 1.07 on fold 1, and from 0.63 to 0.72 and from 0.94 to 1.00 on fold 2. Part of each team rating is home-arena scorer recording.

**5. Time-forward persistence** (the B1p coefficient; B0 offset; games t+1..t+20, SE clustered by team-season):

| | own (for) | opp (against) |
|---|---|---|
| fold 1 | 0.652 (0.119) | 1.054 (0.079) |
| fold 2 | 0.714 (0.088) | 0.976 (0.081) |
| lag 0, fold 2 | 0.726 | 0.994 |
| "against" by season, fwd | 2021-22 1.17, 2022-23 0.98, 2023-24 0.84 | |

The decay from lag 1 to lag 20 is flat ("against" 0.99 -> 0.99; "for" 0.75 -> 0.69). The rating does not go stale within 20 games.

**Cause (stated plainly).** The over-response is non-stationarity across seasons, not a fitting artefact.
- The persistence of the defending-team block rating falls every training season: 1.15 -> 1.01 -> 0.87 at lag 0. The arena recording effect falls with it (0.75 -> 0.20).
- A coefficient pooled over training seasons estimates the average persistence, about 0.95-1.0, and is applied to a test season that sits further down the trend. This is the L27 mechanism (a pooled offset under a trend), acting on a slope instead of a level.
- Fold 1 sits at the edge of the band: 0.99 fitted against 2023-24's 0.85 gives a predicted ratio of about 1.17, and 1.18 was observed. Fold 2 applies 0.94 to 2024-25. Continuing the trend, about 0.7 is expected there, which gives about 1.3-1.5 (observed 1.49).
- A smaller, constant part comes from rating maturity (the early-season slope is about 0.6-0.8x the pooled slope).

**Implication for the pre-registered arms.**
- B1p's persistence (fold 2: 0.71 / 0.98) is no smaller than B1's coefficients (0.63 / 0.94). B1p is estimated without the arena factor, so if anything it responds more. It is expected not to fix fold 2.
- B1m addresses only the maturity part, which is constant across seasons.
- B2p inherits B1p's scale.
- The arms are run as written (brief); the expectation is recorded here, before any step-1 fit.
